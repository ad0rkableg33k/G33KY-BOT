4. Camera Policy

When enabled by a server administrator, the Bot may monitor whether a member's camera is enabled in designated voice channels. The default system provides a silent grace period, a warning/reminder, a configurable countdown, and removal if the requirement isn't met.

The default configuration is a 2-minute silent grace period followed by a 3-minute warning period, though server administrators may change these settings. Members with designated exemption roles are excluded from camera enforcement.

The Bot does not record, save, or transmit camera video. It uses Discord's voice-state information only to determine whether the camera requirement is satisfied.

5. Exemptions
