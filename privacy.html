Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026  ·  Last Updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how High-Speed Connection Bot ("HSC Bot," "we," "us," or "our") — also known as G33KY Bot — collects, uses, stores, and protects information when the Bot is used in a Discord server.

1. Information We Collect

The Bot collects only information reasonably necessary to provide its configured features. Depending on which features a server administrator enables, this may include:

Discord Account Information

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord server/guild ID
  • Server membership information
  • Discord roles
  • Username or display information when required for a command

Camera Status — when the Camera Policy is enabled, the Bot receives relevant Discord voice-state information to determine whether a member's camera is enabled. The Bot does not record or store the member's actual camera video.

2. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The Bot does not intentionally collect or store:

  • Camera recordings, microphone recordings, or audio recordings
  • Copies of members' message content
  • Private messages
  • Passwords or Discord account credentials
  • Payment card information

3. How We Use Information

Information collected by the Bot is used to:

  • Determine whether camera requirements are satisfied
  • Send camera reminders and remove members from monitored voice channels when configured
  • Provide administrator commands
  • Maintain server-specific configuration
  • Diagnose technical problems and maintain the Bot

4. Server-Specific Configuration

The Bot stores configuration separately for each Discord server, keyed by server ID.

Camera configuration may include: whether the camera policy is enabled, monitored voice channels, exempt roles, grace-period and warning-period settings, and the policy announcement link.

5. Data Storage

The Bot currently stores configuration information in local application data files, which may include:

  • camera-config.json
  • channels.json
  • descriptions.json

The location of these files depends on the hosting environment. If the Bot is hosted using a third-party provider, that provider may have access to the infrastructure on which the Bot operates, subject to that provider's own policies.

6. Discord and Third-Party Services

The Bot relies on Discord's API and infrastructure. Information transmitted through Discord is also subject to Discord's own policies. The Bot may also rely on third-party hosting or infrastructure providers, who may process information as necessary to provide hosting, networking, storage, monitoring, or other infrastructure services.

7. Data Retention

Server configuration data is retained for as long as the Bot is active in a server. Removing the Bot from a server does not automatically delete stored configuration. Requests for deletion may be sent to dragon.exe@atomicmail.io.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information processed by the Bot. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You acknowledge that use of Internet-based services carries inherent security risks.

9. Server Administrators

The administrator of a Discord server decides whether to enable the Bot's features and determines how those features are configured. Members with questions about why the Bot is being used in a particular server should first contact that server's administrators.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information, including rights to:

  • Request access to information associated with you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request additional information about how your information is processed

Because the Bot operates within Discord servers controlled by independent administrators, some requests may need to be directed to the relevant server administrator. Requests concerning the Bot's own processing may be sent to: dragon.exe@atomicmail.io

11. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not specifically directed toward children. You must comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws when using Discord and the Bot. If we become aware that we have collected personal information in violation of applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices, features, or legal obligations change. The current version will be available at this URL.

13. Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to: dragon.exe@atomicmail.io
